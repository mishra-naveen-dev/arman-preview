About Arman

An RBI-registered NBFC serving rural and semi-urban India since 1992.

Arman means aspiration, or wish. The company was incorporated on 26 November 1992 as Arman Lease & Finance Ltd. and made its public offering on 21 August 1995, when the issue was subscribed 22 times. Its shares trade on BSE (531179) and NSE (ARMANFIN).

Arman offers MSME, two-wheeler, solar and loan against property finance. Group-based and individual microfinance runs through Namra Finance Limited, a wholly owned subsidiary registered with RBI as an NBFC-MFI.

All operations are in-house, with bottom-up credit appraisal and collection practices built for the areas the company serves.

In memory of Mr. Jayendra B. Patel
